Frequently Asked Questions

Is CityRideTaxi an aggregator like Uber or Ola? expand_more

No. NJJ Technologies does not operate a consumer hailing service. We engineer the complete white-label software platform for transportation companies, fleet owners, and taxi associations who retain 100% control over their rider relationships and revenues.

How fast is the turnkey white-label deployment? expand_more

Because CityRideTaxi is a production-proven system currently live on cityridetaxi.org, complete white-label deployments (with your logo, brand colors, custom app store accounts, and tariff policies) typically roll out within 2 to 3 weeks.

Can we configure custom regional tariff rules? expand_more

Yes. The dynamic tariff engine allows you to define base rates, minimum distance guarantees (e.g. 130 km outstation rule), waiting surcharges (₹2/min), driver betta allowances, hill-station topography fees, and pass-through toll calculations.

How are multi-vendor margin splits settled? expand_more

Our automated ledger calculates platform take-rate, vendor fleet commission, and net driver pay upon every single trip completion, generating exportable statements with automatic GST reconciliation.
