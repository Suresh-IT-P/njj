Color

A near-black and cool-neutral base with a single accent, route teal, reserved for CTAs, links, live data and selection. Semantic colors are used only for status. Ink chapters stay dark in both themes.

Motion

Motion explains state: live data moves, connections carry flow, controls respond. All motion stops under reduced-motion settings.

--t-fast · 140msHover, press, color
--t-base · 240msNav, dropdown, card lift
--t-slow · 480msAccordion, chart draw

Easing: --ease cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.7, 0.2, 1) · --ease-in-out cubic-bezier(0.65, 0, 0.35, 1)
