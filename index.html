Live · Rider App

Customer Web & Mobile App

Consumer-grade booking interface for local city trips, inter-city outstation travel, and on-demand parcel deliveries.

  • 3 Travel Categories: Local rides, Outstation (One-Way min 130 km, Round-Trip toggle with return date), and Parcel dispatch.
  • Parcel Logistics: 3 tiered weight categories (<5kg Bike, 5-15kg Auto, 15-30kg Cab) with sender & receiver contact verification.
  • Interactive Map Picker: Crosshair pin with automated reverse geocoding and live location detection.
  • Multi-Passenger Capacity: Flexible seat allocation from 1 up to 12 Passengers per booking.
  • Wallet & Promo Engine: In-app digital wallet, coupon discounts (e.g. WELCOME50), live tracking, and printable PDF invoices.

Driver Command Center

Dedicated driver partner cockpit with real-time radar scanning, audio-visual ride ping overlays, and earnings ledgers.

Partner Hub (Vendor Portal)

Enterprise portal empowering fleet operators, taxi vendors, and vehicle suppliers to manage their mini-fleets and settlements.

  • Sub-Fleet Vehicle Onboarding: Register and maintain vehicles across categories (Sedan, SUV, EV, Auto, Commercial).
